## Archived: 2026-10-31

* **Repository Context:**
  * Project: RepRap Ltd Electric 3D Printer research project.
  * Concept: Combines reverse-CT scan 3D printing, electric 3D scanning, and electropolymerisation.
  * Goal: Use electric current to polymerise liquid monomer into a 3D shape in a single scan without moving parts.
  * Current Progress:
    * 2D and 3D simulations solving Laplace's equation for field and current distribution.
    * Employing marching cubes algorithm for converting charge distributions to 3D shapes.
    * Experimenting with Neural Networks and GANs to generate voltage distributions for controlled shapes.
  * Repository Structure:
    * `README.md`: Contains the ongoing research logs and theories.
    * `Software/`: Holds the codebase including `GAN/`, `OpenEIT/`, `PySimulation/`, and `Simulation/`.
